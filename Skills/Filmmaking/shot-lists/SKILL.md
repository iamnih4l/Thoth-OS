# Shot Lists

## Purpose
Organize production shooting with comprehensive shot-by-shot technical specifications.

## When to Activate
- Preparing for video shoots
- Coordinating with camera operators
- Production planning

## When NOT to Activate
- Improvised or documentary-style shoots
- Post-production only tasks

## Workflow
1. **Understand** - Analyze requirements
2. **Research** - Gather relevant information
3. **Plan** - Design the approach
4. **Execute** - Produce the deliverable
5. **Review** - Quality check and iterate
6. **Deliver** - Finalize and document
