# Blog Writing

## Purpose
Create blog content that educates, engages, and ranks well in search engines.

## When to Activate
- Publishing technical insights
- Content marketing
- Documentation in blog format

## When NOT to Activate
- Social media posts (use specific platform skill)
- Academic writing

## Workflow
1. **Understand** - Analyze requirements
2. **Research** - Gather relevant information
3. **Plan** - Design the approach
4. **Execute** - Produce the deliverable
5. **Review** - Quality check and iterate
6. **Deliver** - Finalize and document

## Checklist
- [ ] Objectives clearly defined
- [ ] Target audience identified
- [ ] Best practices applied

- [ ] Output reviewed and polished
