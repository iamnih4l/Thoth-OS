# Experiment Design

## Purpose
Create well-structured experiments that produce reliable, actionable results with minimal bias.

## When to Activate
- Testing new approaches or technologies
- A/B testing design
- Feature validation experiments

## When NOT to Activate
- Decisions that don't need experimental validation
- Tasks with clear, known solutions

## Workflow
1. **Understand** - Analyze requirements
2. **Research** - Gather relevant information
3. **Plan** - Design the approach
4. **Execute** - Produce the deliverable
5. **Review** - Quality check and iterate
6. **Deliver** - Finalize and document
