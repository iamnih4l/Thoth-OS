# Literature Review

## Purpose
Survey existing research comprehensively to identify the state-of-the-art, gaps, and opportunities.

## When to Activate
- Starting research in a new area
- Writing research papers
- Technology evaluation
- Understanding domain state-of-the-art

## When NOT to Activate
- Well-understood topics with established solutions
- Time-sensitive decisions where review is impractical

## Workflow
1. **Understand** - Analyze requirements
2. **Research** - Gather relevant information
3. **Plan** - Design the approach
4. **Execute** - Produce the deliverable
5. **Review** - Quality check and iterate
6. **Deliver** - Finalize and document
