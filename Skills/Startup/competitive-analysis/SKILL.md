# Competitive Analysis

## Purpose
Evaluate the competitive landscape to inform product strategy, positioning, and differentiation.

## When to Activate
- Entering a new market
- Competitor launches a new feature
- Strategic planning sessions

## When NOT to Activate
- No competitors exist (blue ocean)
- Internal tools with no market competition
