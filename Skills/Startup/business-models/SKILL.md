# Business Models

## Purpose
Design sustainable business models that align value creation with revenue generation.

## When to Activate
- Starting a new venture
- Pivoting business model
- Investor preparation

## When NOT to Activate
- Technical implementation tasks
- Established business models needing no changes

2. **Research** - Gather relevant information
3. **Plan** - Design the approach
4. **Execute** - Produce the deliverable
5. **Review** - Quality check and iterate
6. **Deliver** - Finalize and document
