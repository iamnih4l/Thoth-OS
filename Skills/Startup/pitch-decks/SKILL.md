# Pitch Decks

## Purpose
Build investor-ready pitch presentations that communicate vision, traction, and opportunity effectively.

## When to Activate
- Fundraising preparation
- Demo day presentations
- Partnership proposals

## When NOT to Activate
- Internal presentations
- Technical architecture decks

## Workflow
1. **Understand** - Analyze requirements
2. **Research** - Gather relevant information
3. **Plan** - Design the approach
4. **Execute** - Produce the deliverable
5. **Review** - Quality check and iterate
6. **Deliver** - Finalize and document
