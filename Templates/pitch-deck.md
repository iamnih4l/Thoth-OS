# Pitch Deck Outline

## 1. Title Slide
- Company Name & Logo
- High-concept pitch (One sentence)

## 2. The Problem
- Describe the pain point
- Who has it? How is it solved today?

## 3. The Solution
- How you solve the problem
- Value proposition

## 4. Why Now?
- Market timing, technological shifts, or regulatory changes

## 5. Market Size
- TAM (Total Addressable Market)
- SAM (Serviceable Available Market)
- SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market)

## 6. Product
- Core features and demo/screenshots

## 7. Business Model
- How you make money (Pricing, LTV, CAC)

## 8. Traction
- Metrics, revenue, users, partnerships

## 9. Competition
- Competitive matrix (Why you are better)

## 10. Team
- Key founders and their relevant experience

## 11. Financials
- 3-5 year projections and key assumptions

## 12. The Ask
- How much are you raising?
- What will you achieve with this capital? (Milestones)
